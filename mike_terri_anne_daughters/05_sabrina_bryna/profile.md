- **Name:** Sabrina Bryna

- **Birth order:** 5th child of Mike and Terri Anne
- **Mother:** Terri Anne Key

- **Conditions:** Cerebral palsy and deafness.

## The Doctor’s Diagnosis: Sabrina Bryna Is Totally Deaf

- After the audiotherapy appointment, Mike and Terri Anne have the next appointment with the doctor.
- The doctor tells Mike and Terri Anne that Sabrina Bryna is totally deaf.
- The doctor tells Mike and Terri Anne what their options are for Sabrina Bryna.
- Terri Anne feels a little bit guilty because she was kidnapped and her left foot was cut off while she was pregnant with Sabrina Bryna. She worries that this trauma may be connected to Sabrina Bryna being totally deaf. This should be handled non-graphically as trauma and danger.
- In the doctor’s office, Mike quickly kneels down and tells Terri Anne that she should not feel that guilty because they have Sabrina Bryna and love her so much, and that they will do anything to communicate with Sabrina Bryna.
- Terri Anne becomes very emotional.
- The doctor presents Mike and Terri Anne with options for Sabrina Bryna:
  - **American Sign Language (ASL)** — a full visual language that lets Sabrina Bryna communicate completely.
  - **Cochlear implants** — devices that can give a sense of sound, usually paired with speech therapy.
  - **Hearing aids** — amplification, though less effective for total deafness.
  - **Speech and listening therapy** — an oral approach focused on speaking and listening, often used with hearing technology.
  - **A combined “total communication” approach.**
- Aldoma Kowalski takes Mike and Terri Anne’s older four daughters — Patti Anne, Jessica Isabella, Rachel Darcy, and Kiley Rose — to the Miami Children’s Museum while Mike and Terri Anne are at the appointment. The family is still in Miami.
- After the appointment, Mike and Terri Anne tell Aldoma about Sabrina Bryna — that she is totally deaf.
- Mike and Terri Anne take a week to consider their options for Sabrina Bryna, talking about the options together.
- After a week of considering the options, Mike and Terri Anne ultimately choose **American Sign Language (ASL)** for Sabrina Bryna.
