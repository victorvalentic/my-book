# Island Survival Storyline (Cast Away-style)

> Part of the Mike & Terri Anne Key outline. Return to the [main outline](mike_terri_anne_key_profile_outline.md).

## Premise

A survival storyline in the style of the movie *Cast Away*: four people are trapped on a deserted island.

## Backstory (before the island) — how Paul came into Klaudia and Langston Siobhan’s lives

- Klaudia Bianca and Langston Siobhan are already living in Australia, staying in Hobart, Tasmania, where Klaudia Bianca is attending university to become a doctor. Emiliano was studying to be a doctor too.
- Klaudia Bianca knows that Emiliano (her partner, Langston Siobhan’s father) had some family in Brisbane.
- Emiliano dies in a terrible vehicle accident when Langston Siobhan is eighteen months old. Langston Siobhan is in the accident too, but her car seat saves her. Emiliano’s parents are also in the accident and are killed.
- After Emiliano’s death, Klaudia Bianca contacts Paul (Emiliano’s family in Brisbane). This is how Paul — and his sailboat — come into Klaudia and Langston Siobhan’s lives, eventually leading to the island.
- **The first visit to Paul’s sailboat:** When Klaudia contacts Paul, he invites Klaudia and Langston Siobhan to visit him and Keagan for a weekend. Racquel Rahiq comes along to give Klaudia support after Emiliano’s death. Keagan and Paul are on Paul’s sailboat, and the women — Klaudia and Racquel — have to take off their shoes and socks to walk on the sailboat, which they have no problem doing out of respect for Paul’s sailboat.
- **The shoes question:** Klaudia asks Paul whether she should remove Langston Siobhan’s shoes and socks too. Paul says no — Langston Siobhan needs her shoes for steadiness on the sailboat — but he thanks Klaudia and says he appreciates the question a lot. When Racquel asks why, Paul explains that most adults with little children around Langston Siobhan’s and Keagan’s ages would not think to ask that kind of question.
- **Paul recognizes Racquel Rahiq:** Paul recognizes Racquel Rahiq from Gotham City, one of Australia’s top-rate brothels, where she works. Paul used to visit Gotham City before he had Keagan to take care of.
- **Racquel recognizes Paul too:** Racquel recognizes Paul because she was the prostitute who slept with him at Gotham City. This history comes up between them.
- **Klaudia knows about Racquel’s work:** Klaudia knows that Racquel works at a brothel, because they are best friends who tell each other everything — they even shared a bedroom when Klaudia’s parents died.
- **The adults discuss the brothel privately:** When the kids are sleeping, the adults — Paul, Klaudia, and Racquel — talk about the brothel (Gotham City).
- After the discussion, Racquel Rahiq joins Paul in his bedroom for a personal connection on his sailboat. Racquel is not working at Gotham City this weekend, so their personal connection is real — not a transaction. (Non-graphic, consenting adults.)
- **Keagan walks in:** In the morning, little Keagan walks into Paul’s bedroom and sees Racquel Rahiq in Paul’s bed.
- Both Paul and Racquel Rahiq are sleeping when Keagan walks into the bedroom. They explain the situation to him in a loving and caring way.
- **Breakfast and a day sail:** Paul makes breakfast for everyone. After breakfast, Paul takes the group out on a sail around the harbor for the day.
- **Child safety on the sail:** Klaudia Bianca and Paul put the children into personal safety harnesses, so the children can walk around the deck freely but cannot walk to the edge and fall overboard into the water.
- **Dinner and back to the sailboat:** After the sail, Paul takes the group to a restaurant for dinner (pizza). Back on the sailboat, they go to their individual bedrooms.
- **Racquel and Klaudia’s night talk:** Racquel Rahiq and Klaudia Bianca share a bed and spend half the night talking about Racquel’s boyfriend, Adolf. Adolf knows Racquel works at Gotham City as a prostitute and accepts it because he loves her so much. Racquel tells Klaudia she does not know whether she will tell Adolf about Paul.
- **The departure:** The next day, Racquel Rahiq, Klaudia Bianca, and Langston Siobhan leave Paul’s sailboat. Racquel goes home to Melbourne; Klaudia and Langston go home to Hobart.
- **Racquel’s guilt:** Racquel feels like she broke a promise — not to sleep with anyone outside of Gotham City — by sleeping with Paul on the sailboat.
- **Klaudia’s promise:** Klaudia Bianca promises Paul that she and Langston Siobhan will visit again in the near future, because of the cousins’ future — so Langston Siobhan and Keagan can grow up knowing each other.
- **Racquel waits for Adolf:** Racquel Rahiq is at home in Melbourne, waiting for Adolf to come home.
- **Adolf comes home to Racquel crying:** Adolf comes home and finds Racquel Rahiq sitting at the kitchen table, crying softly. Adolf asks why she is crying when she has just had a beautiful weekend with Klaudia Bianca and Langston Siobhan on Paul’s sailboat, and he also asks about Langston Siobhan and Keagan.
- **Racquel confesses, and Adolf forgives:** Racquel tells Adolf that the kids had a great time, but Paul was one of her customers at Gotham City — and she did not know that before the trip. On Friday night, Paul invited her to join him in his bed. Racquel says she is sorry for cheating outside of the brothel. Adolf forgives Racquel because he loves her so much.
- **Adolf’s trust and Racquel’s uncertainty:** Adolf tells Racquel that if she wants to go back to the brothel, he does not mind, because it is her own body. He trusts her fully. Racquel tells him that she does not know what to do.
- **Racquel asks why Adolf is so forgiving:** Racquel is really surprised by Adolf’s forgiving reaction to her cheating, and asks him why he is so forgiving. Adolf tells her he is a psychology student who totally understands her sexual behaviors and motivations — which stem from a lack of a father figure in her life.
- **Adolf takes Racquel out for a romantic dinner:** Adolf takes Racquel Rahiq out to a restaurant for a very romantic dinner.

## How they got trapped

Paul's sailboat is caught in a very awful storm, leaving the four of them stranded on the island.
- **Timeline anchor:** Klaudia Bianca and Racquel Rahiq are both twenty-four years old when the sailboat disappears. Since Klaudia and Racquel were both born in 2066, this places the disappearance around 2090. Langston Siobhan is six at the time, which means Klaudia had her at eighteen.
- **Uncle Paul:** The kids all call Paul “Uncle Paul.” Because the kids’ clothes were lost in the ocean during the storm, Uncle Paul lets them go free — go without clothes — on the island. (Non-sexual, consistent with the story’s nudist theme.)
- **Uncle Paul gives up on clothes after the first month:** For the first month, Uncle Paul tries to make the kids wear clothes, but then he gives up, and the kids go without clothes on the island.
- **Survival skills:** Uncle Paul teaches the boys — Keagan and Virgil — how to hunt and fish on the island.
- **Feeding Langston Siobhan first:** Uncle Paul teaches the boys that the food they catch should go to Langston Siobhan, so the boys provide for her first.
- **Langston Siobhan’s nightly tears, and the boys’ comfort:** Every night, Langston Siobhan cries herself to sleep, missing Andrea Jennifer and Klaudia Bianca. The boys feel sorry for her — she has become their friend — and, feeling helpless, they ask Uncle Paul how to help. Uncle Paul tells them they are already helping her just by being her friends. One night, the boys sneak into her bed to comfort her, and she stops crying. (Innocent, non-sexual comfort between the children.)
- **Langston Siobhan wanders off to swim:** Langston Siobhan wanders off alone to swim in a private lagoon, and Uncle Paul is angry with her for wandering off to go swimming.
- **The reconciliation (three days later):** Langston Siobhan tries to apologize to Uncle Paul for days, and his heart slowly melts toward the little girl in his care. On the third day, Uncle Paul kneels down to her eye level, forgives her, and hugs her and kisses her cheeks. She hugs him and kisses his cheeks too, then runs off to play with Keagan and Virgil.
- **The carefree day (same day as the reconciliation):** On the same day, Uncle Paul finds the kids sitting on a tree branch, talking to each other, and he laughs out loud at how carefree they are, with no worries in the world.
- **Uncle Paul’s death (a week after the reconciliation, about nine months in):** A week later — about nine months into their time on the island — Uncle Paul dies suddenly from a ruptured brain aneurysm (a massive brain bleed). Langston Siobhan feels guilty for his death and becomes very emotional. With Uncle Paul gone, the three children — Langston Siobhan, Keagan, and Virgil — are left alone on the island.
- **Uncle Paul’s funeral — Langston Siobhan’s dress:** The children hold a funeral for Uncle Paul. Langston Siobhan gets dressed for it, but her summer dress is too small for her now — she has outgrown it during their months on the island.
- The boys’ clothes are too small for them too, like Langston Siobhan’s dress.
- **Birthdays on the island:** Langston Siobhan and Keagan each turn another year older on the island (Langston Siobhan turns seven, and Keagan turns eight).
- **They cannot bury Uncle Paul:** The kids have no shovels to dig the ground, so they have to leave Uncle Paul’s body on the sand.
- **Moving to the other side of the island:** Langston Siobhan asks Keagan and Virgil to go to the other side of the island — away from Uncle Paul’s body — and they agree. They use their rowboat to cross to the other side.
- **The rowboat (attached to the sailboat):** The rowboat is attached to Uncle Paul’s sailboat and is meant for emergencies only.
- **Setting up camp on the other side:** When they reach the other side of the island, the boys help Langston Siobhan out of the rowboat and walk her to a coconut tree. The boys set up camp — getting a fire lit, finding water, and fishing for food — using the survival skills Uncle Paul taught them.
- **Langston Siobhan refuses to eat:** Langston Siobhan will not eat anything because she is upset and feels guilty about Uncle Paul’s death. The boys grow worried about her safety because of her lack of food intake, and they feel helpless.
- **Langston Siobhan turns a corner:** One day, Langston Siobhan gets up, walks over to Keagan and Virgil, and has a good cry on their shoulders. She even tries to eat a little bit of coconut — the first sign she is coming back to them.
- **Langston Siobhan goes quiet:** Langston Siobhan becomes very quiet, and the boys are very nervous for her.

- **Keagan** — 7 years old. Paul's nephew (Keagan does not know who his own parents are), and Langston Siobhan's second cousin.
- **Virgil** — 7 years old. Keagan's best friend.
- **Paul** — 50 years old. Owns the sailboat; Keagan's uncle.
