## Key Corporation

Key Corporation is a massive mixed global empire with many divisions. Because it is not limited to one industry, it can touch nearly every part of the story: money, real estate, media, security, healthcare, technology, finance, nightlife, legal power, and political influence.

### Story Uses

- It explains why Mike is the wealthiest man in the world.
- It gives Mike’s siblings a powerful reason to fight him.
- It gives Randy access to Mike’s world because Randy serves as one of Key Corporation’s vice presidents.
- It creates corporate-thriller conflict: surveillance, records, contracts, firings, lawsuits, blackmail, and inheritance battles.
- It makes the Blue Velvet Room more dangerous because it may be one hidden piece of a much larger empire.

## Villains, Rivals, and Threats

### Candice Key

Candice Key, Mike’s mother, is a major antagonist even if she is not physically present in the main story. Her will controls the family from the background and forces Mike to marry and have a child before his fortieth birthday — if he fails, Key Corporation passes to his siblings (Curtis, Jack, Gloria, Ava, and Victoria), but not to Ashlee.

#### Threats Created by Her Will

- Mike could lose Key Corporation to his siblings (but not to Ashlee) if he does not marry and have a child before his fortieth birthday.
- The siblings may use the will to challenge Mike’s authority.
- Terri Anne may be treated as a business solution rather than a wife.
- Ashlee’s exclusion from the will exposes Candice’s cruelty toward Ashlee for being both transgender and disabled.

### The Five Siblings: Curtis, Jack, Gloria, Ava, and Victoria

Mike’s five siblings — **Curtis, Jack, Gloria, Ava, and Victoria** — are his villains and rivals. They were cut out of Key Corporation, resent Mike for inheriting power, and are in prison. They are mean and very angry, with Victoria standing out as especially mean and angry. They wanted to get rid of Mike and were behind the assassination attempt that injured Ashlee while she was horseback riding with him. Victoria led the plan, and Candice did not know anything about it.

#### Victoria’s Role in the Assassination Attempt

Victoria led the plan to have Mike killed. Curtis, Jack, Gloria, and Ava were also involved, but Victoria was the driving force. Candice did not know anything about the plan. Victoria later gets into Ashlee and Geoffery’s Los Angeles mansion at 3:00 in the morning using the mansion pass key code that Daniela gave her. Victoria spills everything to them in their bedroom about the assassination plot. Because there are no cameras in the bedroom, the confession creates a dramatic evidence problem. Victoria also breaks their iPhones, Ashlee’s wheelchair, and Ashlee’s special wind pedals for her grand piano, trapping them without an easy way to call Mike. Ashlee and Geoffery prove what happened through their testimony and evidence from the break-in, unauthorized pass key access, and sabotage. Mike is already on his way to visit them, flying from Carlisle, Ontario, to Los Angeles on one of Key Corporation’s planes. This confession becomes the key break in the case and helps explain how the siblings are exposed and sent to prison. Six months later, police arrest Daniela, and Daniela tells them everything, including that she gave Victoria the mansion pass key code, giving the police another insider account of Victoria’s scheme. This makes Victoria one of Mike’s most dangerous siblings and explains why she is especially mean and angry.

#### Victoria’s Wallace-Harper-Clein Scheme (from prison)

Even from prison, Victoria continues to work against Mike. She hires Tom Wallace (the Blue Velvet Room manager), Don Clein (the bouncer), and Cooper Harper (Mike’s own limousine driver), who are related. Her plan is for Cooper, who is close to Mike, to break up Mike’s relationships, so that Mike cannot marry and have a child before his fortieth birthday. If Mike fails that condition of Candice’s will, Key Corporation passes to Mike’s siblings (including Victoria) and not to Ashlee. Mike keeps Cooper employed on purpose, to trace the scheme back to Victoria and keep his enemy close.

- Regain control of Key Corporation
- Punish Mike for being chosen
- Expose Mike’s ownership of the Blue Velvet Room
- Humiliate Terri Anne publicly
- Use Patti Anne’s mystery/twist against the family
- Challenge Mike and Terri Anne’s marriage
- Attack Mike through the people he loves
- Arrange or support an assassination attempt against Mike
- Be exposed through Victoria’s confession at Ashlee and Geoffery’s mansion

- Use Daniela, Ashlee’s Personal Support Worker, as a hidden ally inside Ashlee’s life
- Use the mansion pass key code Daniela provides to enter Ashlee and Geoffery’s mansion
