## Short Story Summary

In November 2023, Mike Key, the thirty-eight-year-old CEO of Key Corporation — a massive mixed global empire — and the wealthiest man in the world, meets eighteen-year-old Terri Anne at the Blue Velvet Room. Terri Anne is a young mother with a very dark backstory, trying to protect her infant daughter, Patti Anne, and escape poverty. She does not know that Mike secretly owns the club where she works.

Mike is also haunted by the fact that Ashlee was shot during an assassination attempt meant for Mike, arranged by the siblings who wanted to get rid of him, with Victoria leading the plan and Candice unaware of it. Victoria later gets into Ashlee and Geoffery’s Los Angeles mansion at 3:00 in the morning using the mansion pass key code Daniela gave her, then spills everything in their bedroom, where there are no cameras. She also breaks their iPhones, Ashlee’s wheelchair, and Ashlee’s special wind pedals for her grand piano, forcing them to wait for Ashlee’s Personal Support Worker, Daniela, before they can call Mike. Daniela is very nervous and does her job anyway. Ashlee puts it all together and quickly calls Mike to warn him about Daniela, and Mike calls the police. Six months later, police arrest Daniela, and she tells them everything, including that she gave Victoria the pass key code. Ashlee and Geoffery’s testimony, evidence from the break-in and sabotage, and Daniela’s later confession expose the truth while Mike is on his way from Carlisle, Ontario, to Los Angeles on one of Key Corporation’s planes. Mike is racing against the terms of his evil mother Candice’s will. If he does not marry and have a child before forty, Key Corporation passes to his siblings (but not to Ashlee). What begins as a complicated connection inside a world of money, secrecy, and survival becomes a real romance, though Terri Anne’s discovery of Mike’s ownership of the Blue Velvet Room complicates their trust. Terri Anne sees Mike as caring and loving, while Mike sees Terri Anne as strong, mature, and independent.

Their relationship is morally complex but deeply emotional. Mike marries Terri Anne on June 22, 2024, and becomes Patti Anne’s adoptive father. But Patti Anne’s biological father, Randy — Key Corporation’s fifty-year-old Vice President of Human Resources — threatens to pull Terri Anne’s darkest past into the future — especially when Randy’s daughter Emma eventually becomes Jessica Isabella’s romantic girlfriend. Randy’s hatred of lesbians leads him to reject Emma, attack Jessica Isabella’s fitness as a mother, and lose his place at Key Corporation because Mike refuses to allow someone with Randy’s prejudice to work in his empire. Over the years, Mike and Terri Anne build a large family together, including identical conjoined twins Jessica Isabella and Rachel Darcy, Kiley Rose, Sabrina Bryna, Fallon Glenda, Andrea Jennifer, and Ashley Erin, who is born as a boy with the birth name Mikkel James Junior. Patti Anne, who has Bipolar Disorder through her biological father, also has a complicated future when she has four children: Gamaliel Galvin, whose father is an unknown random man from Patti Anne’s survival-sex period on the streets of Rio de Janeiro while Patti Anne is not taking medication for her Bipolar Disorder; Klara, whom Patti Anne fosters after Klara tells Gamaliel Galvin that her father has been touching her, Gamaliel Galvin vomits his dinner, and Gamaliel Galvin tells Mike, who quickly calls the OPP on Klara’s father before the OPP raid Klara’s home and arrest him; Klara spends two months in a temporary foster home with Rose, Teal, Monyka, and Baby Lily while Patti Anne’s paperwork is completed, where Rose inappropriately touches Klara while the blind foster mother does not know what Rose is doing; when Klara moves into Patti Anne’s home she is quiet, angry, jumpy, and withdrawn, worries about losing her Victoria Ballet Academy spot, and is reassured by Patti Anne that her spot is safe, but she slowly gets used to living with Patti Anne and Gamaliel Galvin, while Gamaliel Galvin treats her like a sister and is protective and gentle; on the school-clothes shopping day, a detective comes to Patti Anne’s mansion because of Klara’s father and brings Klara’s belongings, including her mother’s things; Klara becomes very emotional when she gets her mother’s necklace back, Patti Anne helps her put it on, Gamaliel Galvin says it is one of the most beautiful things he has ever seen, Patti Anne and the detective speak privately about Klara’s father’s court case, and the detective tells Patti Anne that he feels sorry for Klara because Klara will have to testify against her father, who has pleaded not guilty to fourteen charges, and Patti Anne takes Gamaliel Galvin and Klara to buy dancewear at a ballet store and school uniforms at the uniform store; Klara chooses long-sleeved bodysuits instead of leotards and white nylons, then sees her psychologist that afternoon because it is the Thursday before school starts on Tuesday; when Klara returns to Victoria Ballet Academy, Patti Anne and Klara’s psychologist tell her she can leave the classroom if she needs to, that she is very strong, and that she should live every second in the classroom, while Gamaliel Galvin promises he will be there if she is very nervous; Patti Anne also tries taking Gamaliel Galvin and Klara to the CN Tower and the Spaghetti Factory in July, but when Klara becomes frightened by the crowds at the top of the CN Tower, Patti Anne immediately removes them, reassures Klara that it is not her fault, and arranges Spaghetti Factory dinner at the mansion instead; Klara has bad nightmares every week and Patti Anne calms her by singing; another week later, Patti Anne takes Gamaliel Galvin and Klara to Mike and Terri Anne’s cottage in the north of Ontario, where Patti Anne reassures Klara that she is allowed to come even though she is a foster daughter, and Gamaliel Galvin tells Klara they can look at millions of stars and play board games; during the cottage vacation, Klara asks about her mother’s photograph and necklace, Patti Anne promises to call the police when they return to the mansion, and later a detective comes to Patti Anne’s mansion because of Klara’s father with Klara’s belongings and her mother’s things, while Gamaliel Galvin walks out of the bathroom in his blue pyjamas during the cottage conversation;
