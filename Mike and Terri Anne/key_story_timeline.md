## Story Timeline Outline

### Before November 2023

- **March 7, 2005:** Terri Anne is born.
- Terri Anne runs away from home at age fifteen.
- Terri Anne does not finish high school.
- Terri Anne becomes involved in prostitution/survival sex during a very dark period of her life. Randy is Patti Anne’s biological father. This should be handled as a serious, non-graphic trauma storyline.
- Mike becomes CEO of Key Corporation, a massive mixed global empire, and grows into the wealthiest man in the world.
- Mike secretly owns the Blue Velvet Room.
- Candice creates a will requiring Mike to marry and have a child before he turns forty on May 31, 2025; if he fails, Key Corporation passes to his siblings (but not to Ashlee).
- Ashlee is written out of the will.
- Mike’s other siblings — Curtis, Jack, Gloria, Ava, and Victoria — are cut out of Key Corporation, become enemies, and are in prison. Their imprisonment is connected to their attempt to get rid of Mike and the assassination attempt that injured Ashlee. Victoria led the plan, and Candice did not know anything about it.
- Victoria later gets into Ashlee and Geoffery’s Los Angeles mansion at 3:00 in the morning using the mansion pass key code that Daniela gave her. Her confession happens in their bedroom, where there are no cameras. She spills everything to Ashlee and Geoffery about the assassination plan. She also breaks their iPhones, Ashlee’s wheelchair, and Ashlee’s special wind pedals for her grand piano. Their testimony and evidence from the break-in, unauthorized pass key access, and sabotage expose the siblings. Mike is on his way to visit Ashlee and Geoffery, flying from Carlisle, Ontario, to Los Angeles on one of Key Corporation’s planes. Because the iPhones are broken, Ashlee and Geoffery have to wait for Ashlee’s Personal Support Worker, Daniela, to come to the mansion so they can call Mike. Daniela is very nervous and does her job anyway. Ashlee puts it all together and quickly calls Mike to warn him about Daniela, and Mike calls the police.
- Six months later, police arrest Daniela. She tells the police everything, including that she gave Victoria the mansion pass key code, giving them inside confirmation of Victoria’s control over her and the mansion betrayal.
