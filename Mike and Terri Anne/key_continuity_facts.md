- **Main story begins:** November 2023.
- **Main locations:** Mike lives in Carlisle, Ontario. Ashlee and Geoffery live in Los Angeles.
- **Key Corporation:** A massive mixed global empire with many divisions.
- **Mike Key:** Born May 31, 1985; 38 years old at the start of the main story; turns forty on May 31, 2025.
- **Terri Anne Key:** Born March 7, 2005; 18 years old at the start of the main story.
- **How they meet:** Mike and Terri Anne meet at the **Blue Velvet Room**.
- **Important secret:** Mike secretly owns the Blue Velvet Room, but Terri Anne does not know this when they meet.
- **How Terri Anne finds out:** Terri Anne brings Mike’s favorite drink, **Johnny Walker on ice**, to the manager’s office and discovers that Mike owns the Blue Velvet Room.
- **Mike’s favorite drink:** Johnny Walker on ice.
- **Mike’s travel to Los Angeles:** Mike is on his way to visit Ashlee and Geoffery and uses one of Key Corporation’s planes to fly from Ontario to Los Angeles.
- **Patti Anne:** Born March 7, 2023, sharing a birthday with Terri Anne. Mike becomes Patti Anne’s adoptive father. Patti Anne’s biological father is **Randy**, who is also the father of **Emma**, Jessica Isabella’s future romantic girlfriend. Patti Anne has Bipolar Disorder through her biological father.
- **Patti Anne’s future children:** In a future adult timeline, Patti Anne has four children: **Gamaliel Galvin**, **Klara**, **Palmer Peter**, and **Salena Sarai**. Patti Anne is nineteen when she has Gamaliel Galvin, whose birthday is January 11. Gamaliel Galvin’s father is an unknown random man. Patti Anne does not know his identity because she has to survive on the streets of Rio de Janeiro and sleeps with different men as survival sex. At this time, Patti Anne is not taking medication to manage her Bipolar Disorder. Klara is the same age as Gamaliel Galvin and her birthday is November 25; Patti Anne becomes Klara’s foster mother when Klara is seven because Klara’s father has been touching her and Klara needs protection. Klara tells Gamaliel Galvin, and Gamaliel Galvin vomits his dinner before telling Mike. Mike quickly calls the OPP on Klara’s father. The OPP raid Klara’s home and arrest her father. Gamaliel Galvin and Klara attend the same school, Victoria Ballet Academy, and Mike is willing to pay Klara’s tuition for her lifetime and for a child psychologist for Klara. Mike does not use other Key family resources beyond tuition, a child psychologist, and backing Patti Anne as Klara’s foster mother. However, Klara has to live in a temporary foster home with four other foster daughters — Rose, Teal, Monyka, and Baby Lily — for two months while the paperwork is completed on Patti Anne. Rose, Teal, and Monyka are hardened, mean, and tough. Rose inappropriately touches Klara; this should be handled non-graphically as abuse/safety danger. Klara’s previous foster mother is blind and does not know what Rose is doing. Baby Lily is only six months old, so her personality is unknown. Klara is quiet, angry, jumpy, and withdrawn when she comes to Patti Anne’s house, but Gamaliel Galvin treats her like a sister and is protective and gentle with her. Klara slowly gets used to living with Patti Anne and Gamaliel Galvin. Her own bedroom and bathroom, ballet, secured tuition, and therapy help her feel safe. Klara calls Patti Anne Patti Anne, and Klara loves Palmer Peter and Salena Sarai. When Patti Anne suggests to Mike that she can become Klara’s foster mother, Mike backs her up without any doubt, even though Patti Anne has Bipolar Disorder, because she is taking medication to help control it. Patti Anne meets **Aleksander Romanova** when she is twenty-nine. Aleksander Romanova’s family is from Kazan, Russia. Aleksander Romanova is the father of Palmer Peter and Salena Sarai. Patti Anne is thirty-two when she has the twins, and they are born when Gamaliel Galvin is twelve years old. Their birthday is July 1, 2055.
