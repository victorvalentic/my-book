## Notes

- I assigned the names based on sound, style, and the second names paired with them.
- Most of the list works best as girl names.
- **Barack Caleb**, **Valera**, and **Viles** work best as boy names.
- **Olya** and **Halyna** have Eastern European/Slavic roots and work well as girl names.
- **Evan**, **Mackenzie Avery**, and **Chase Hannah** have unisex elements, but in this file they are being used as girl names.
